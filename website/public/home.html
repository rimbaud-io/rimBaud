rimBaud is the screen for a fleet of trading bots. One place shows what is running, which side each bot is on, and the knobs that tune it. The bot engine underneath can change. The overview stays.

Live C++ on Linux

See

Home lists every hunter, its side, and whether it is running. The chart draws the coin with the stop, the target, and the trail. Wire shows how a wallet, a coin, and a controller connect.

Tune

BIAS is long, short, or flat. Flat stops the next entry and leaves an open clip in place. Knobs set the take-profit, the trail, and the stop. A saved preset belongs to one hunter.

Keep apart

Each hunter has its own account. The overview does not invent a new order path. Close and a full leftover open are the only one-click size actions. A knob does not flatten a position.

Not one engine

rimBaud is the overview, not the strategy engine. Hummingbot hunters are what this desk watches today. A second engine, such as Freqtrade, can run beside them and report into the same screen. The desk does not have to be that engine.

On the desk

What you work with

  • Spring The local brain that reads and writes hunter files.
  • The native desk and this web desk On the same numbers.
  • A chart per coin Tied to the hunter you pick.
  • A wire canvas Wallet, coin, controller, and bias.

Hummingbots

The hunters this desk watches today

Eight Hummingbot hunters report into the overview. Where a hunter has a bias, LONG or SHORT can open a new clip and FLAT stays idle. An open clip does not flip. A second engine can sit beside them.

⚡

HL Copy

Copy · up to 3 wallets

Mirrors open perpetual positions from up to three Hyperliquid wallets. A new position opens the same side. A close on their book closes the mirror. Two leaders on the same coin: the first one wins. Copy shorts is a controller switch. HL Copy has no bias node.

▤

PMM Simple

BTC · ETH · LTC · TAO

LONG and flat rests one buy with the leftover margin. The clip then holds for the stop, the take-profit, or the trail. SHORT and flat stays idle until a full open. A full open follows the current bias. An open clip is never flattened by the other side.

∿

Helliot

BTC · ETH · LTC · TAO

Elliott structure on 15-minute swings. It looks for a 3-wave and enters in the fib retrace of the last impulse, long or short with the bias. The dashboard full-open buttons skip the wave wait. Exits are the ROE stop, the ROE target, and the structure target.

≈

SuperTrend

BTC · ETH · LTC · TAO

SuperTrend signal, length and multiplier on the controller, with a distance gate so it does not chase. Bias still gates the entry. A SuperTrend reverse does not flip an open clip. Exit is the home stop, close, full open, or a scale-out.

▾

Shorty

BTC · ETH · LTC · TAO

Auto-entry stays short: 15-minute Bollinger high, and the 1-hour average is not up. A full open follows the current bias and does not flip an open clip. After a take-profit it waits for a bounce before it shorts again. The ROE stop covers the open clip.

▦

Dman

BTC · ETH · LTC · TAO

Bollinger DMan. Long when price is through the lower band, short when it is through the upper band, in the bias direction. One clip of leftover margin. Trail plus the home ROE stop. No extra DCA clips on the open. Hold time is 12, 24, or 68 hours.

▲

Only Up

BTC · LTC · TAO

Supertrend rider. Auto-entry stays long: 1-hour average up, 15-minute Supertrend long, and price not extended from the line. A full open follows the current bias and never flips an open clip. At the scale ROE it clips size and trails the runner.

⧉

DMan Maker

BTC · ETH · LTC · TAO · the paper show

Market-opens the leftover margin when bias is LONG or SHORT. It does not wait for a dip. The clip holds for take-profit, trail, or the ROE stop, then can re-enter on the same bias. dontGoCrazy and afterLossNoRebuy sit on this controller. The wire show runs this hunter.

Wallets

One wallet per hunter

A hunter does not trade until it has its own subaccount. The public show never opens those wallets. It uses a paper test account.

⬡

Hunter wallets

HL Copy, PMM Simple, Helliot, SuperTrend, Shorty, Dman, Only Up, and DMan Maker each have a wallet node. The address is configured on the desk, not on this page.

▣

FUNDS bus

One node for every Hyperliquid subaccount on the desk. Wire it when you want the book of balances in the graph.

⛨

RISK wallet

A separate wallet. On the open wire, the controller’s extra out copies every second win here. The address stays on the desk. It sends no orders from this page.

◻

Test account

The wire show only. Paper book, $1,000 start. Equity, free margin, used margin, realized, and open uPnL. No live keys and no orders.

Settings

Stops, moon, and controllers

Wire a stop or a moon into a hunter. Each hunter also has its own controller for trail, rebuy, and clip size.

▼

Stop loss %

ROE exit. Wire it into the hunter SL port. The home default on the desk is −20 ROE. At 40×, about half a percent of price is −20 ROE.

▲

Moon %

A price exit. Wire it into the hunter MOON port. On a long it sells when price is up that far. On a short it covers when price is down that far.

BIAS

LONG, SHORT, FLAT

Bias is the permission to open. FLAT stays idle. Wiring a new bias does not flip a clip that is already open.

⊕

Manual BIAS

LONG, SHORT, or FLAT for every coin. Wire the output into a hunter bias port.

⊕

Desk BIAS

One desk-wide LONG, FLAT, or SHORT. Telegram out goes to the desk. Setting it does not close an open clip.

◈

Hunter BIAS

PMM Simple, Helliot, SuperTrend, Shorty, Dman, Only Up, and DMan Maker each have a bias node for their own coins. HL Copy does not.

Condor

Words in, bias out

Condor is the side door. Telegram and Grok can set a hunter bias. Keys stay on the desk. This site does not ask for them.

⇄

Translator

Turns Telegram words into LONG, SHORT, FLAT, CLOSE, or OPEN 100% on a hunter bias. Coins on the node are BTC, ETH, and LTC.

⏹

Close translator

CLOSE only. An extra Telegram line goes to the hunter emergency close. Idle does nothing.

✈

Telegram bot

Token and chat ids. Log into the bot posts open and close alerts. The bot into Condor carries commands.

✦

Grok bot

Model and key, wired into Condor GROK. The key lives on the desk controller, not on this page.

✉

Email alerts

Health alerts can land here. SMTP is marked later on the desk.
