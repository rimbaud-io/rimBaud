Deutsch

English

This program can send real orders with the keys on this machine.
Perpetual contracts can lose the whole amount in the account. Liquidation can wipe a position.
Freiquell e.V. does not hold the money, does not promise a profit, and is not the exchange.
Only money you can afford to lose belongs in the account.
